## Concepts
- **OTC vs Exchange-Traded Futures** — Distinguishes standardised futures from customisable OTC contracts across size, grade, location and settlement.
- **Central Clearing** — Covers the role of a clearing house in eliminating bilateral counterparty credit risk in OTC markets.
- **Henry Hub** — Describes the physical and contractual primacy of Henry Hub as the benchmark US natural gas trading and delivery point.
- **Natural Gas Forward Curve** — Covers the seasonal shape of natural gas futures prices and non-parallel shifts across the curve.
- **Natural Gas Strips** — Defines calendar, winter and summer strips of consecutive futures contracts used as fixed legs in swaps.
- **Fixed-Price Financial Swap** — Covers the exchange of a fixed futures-settlement price for a floating index price over a delivery period.
- **Basis Swap** — Covers the hedging of geographic price differentials between Henry Hub and a specific delivery location.
- **Swing Swap** — Defines the exchange of a fixed price for a daily published index price, often used for volumetric risk management.
- **Exotic Options in OTC Gas Markets** — Identifies Asian, barrier, spread and swing options available in OTC markets as alternatives to vanilla exchange options.
- **March-April Widowmaker Spread** — Covers the highly volatile intermonth spread between March and April natural gas futures driven by end-of-winter storage dynamics.
- **Natural Gas Volatility** — Characterises natural gas as having consistently high realised and implied volatility relative to other commodity futures.
- **OTC Bilateral vs OTC Cleared Trading** — Distinguishes direct counterparty credit management from centrally cleared OTC execution and their respective risk profiles.
- **ICE Lot Contract Size** — Defines the OTC ICE Lot as 2,500 MMBTU per day, one quarter of the NYMEX futures contract size.
- **Physical Natural Gas Delivery Hubs** — Covers the network of US pipeline delivery points at which fixed-price and basis OTC contracts are settled physically.
- **Price Transparency in OTC Markets** — Covers live anonymous quote dissemination as a mechanism for attracting liquidity and supporting price discovery.
