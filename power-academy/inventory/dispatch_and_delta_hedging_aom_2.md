## Concepts
- **AOM Baseload Delta** — Sensitivity of an at-the-money baseload option position to changes in underlying baseload power prices.
- **Peak Incremental Delta** — Price sensitivity of a peak incremental option or structured product with respect to peak power price movements.
- **Composite Baseload Delta** — Aggregated delta of a combined peak and off-peak position expressed in equivalent baseload terms.
- **Dispatch** — Physical or financial exercise schedule of a generation or structured power asset used to compare against theoretical delta hedges.
- **Delta Hedging** — The practice of offsetting price risk of an option or structured power product by holding a position in the underlying equal to the option's delta.
- **Pre-correction vs Post-correction Delta** — Distinction between raw model-output delta values and those adjusted for known pricing or model errors prior to hedge execution.
- **Negative Composite Baseload Delta** — Edge case where the net baseload-equivalent delta of a composite peak/off-peak position becomes negative, requiring a short hedge.
