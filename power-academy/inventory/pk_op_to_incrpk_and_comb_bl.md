## Concepts
- **Peak (Pk) product** — A power delivery product covering on-peak hours within a scheduling period.
- **Off-Peak (OP) product** — A power delivery product covering off-peak hours within a scheduling period.
- **Incremental Peak (IncPk) product** — A product representing the incremental or differential quantity between two peak-related positions.
- **Base (Base) product** — A flat around-the-clock power delivery product covering all hours in a scheduling period.
- **Product decomposition identity** — The arithmetic relationship expressing how peak, off-peak, and incremental peak quantities combine to replicate a base-load position.
- **Delta (delta) position** — The net incremental exposure arising from combining or stripping standard power products.
