## Concepts
- **Spread option** — An option whose payoff depends on the difference between two underlying asset prices.
- **Kirk's approximation** — A closed-form approximation for pricing spread options by reducing the two-asset problem to a single-asset Black-Scholes framework.
- **Equivalent volatility** — A composite volatility parameter that captures the joint uncertainty of two correlated assets in a spread option formula.
- **Margrabe's formula** — An exact closed-form formula for pricing exchange options (zero-strike spread options) under lognormal dynamics.
- **Bjerksund-Stensland approximation** — An extension of Kirk's formula that provides an improved spread option approximation accounting for correlation structure.
- **Li-Deng-Zhou formula** — An analytical approximation for spread option pricing that refines the equivalent volatility to better reflect inter-asset correlation.
- **Correlation between underlying assets** — The linear dependence parameter between two asset price processes that directly enters spread option valuation formulas.
- **Delta of a spread option** — The sensitivity of a spread option's price to changes in each of the two underlying asset prices.
- **Lognormal asset dynamics** — The assumption that each underlying asset follows geometric Brownian motion, underpinning closed-form spread option approximations.
- **Spark spread / commodity spread** — The difference between electricity prices and fuel costs, commonly structured as a spread option in power markets.
