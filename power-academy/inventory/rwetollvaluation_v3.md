## Concepts
- **Tolling Agreement** — A contractual arrangement where the buyer pays a capacity fee for the right to dispatch a power plant against variable costs.
- **Clean Spark Spread (CSS) Option** — A monthly option on the spread between UK power prices and NBP gas prices net of variable generation costs.
- **Strip of Monthly Options** — A sequence of independent monthly optionality strips used to decompose and value a multi-season tolling deal.
- **Variable Cost (VOM)** — The per-MWh operating cost threshold that determines the in-the-money condition for each monthly option exercise.
- **Capacity Fee** — The fixed periodic premium paid by the buyer of a tolling agreement in exchange for dispatch rights over the contracted capacity.
- **Peak vs Baseload Optionality** — The distinction between options written on peak-hours power delivery and those on round-the-clock baseload delivery, each with different variable cost structures.
- **EFA Season** — The UK electricity market seasonal convention (Winter/Summer) used to define delivery periods in power contracts.
- **Reprice / Mark-to-Market Date** — The reference date on which market inputs are refreshed to produce an updated fair value for bidding or risk reporting purposes.
