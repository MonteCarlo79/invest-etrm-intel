## Concepts
- **Guarantee of Origin (GoO)** — A tradeable certificate attesting that a specified quantity of electricity was generated from a renewable energy source.
- **GoO supply agreement** — A bilateral contract governing the long-term purchase and sale of Guarantees of Origin between a seller and a buyer.
- **Confidential Information** — Any information exchanged between parties in the context of negotiating a GoO or power supply agreement, subject to non-disclosure obligations.
- **Term sheet** — A non-binding document outlining the core commercial terms of a proposed GoO transaction prior to execution of definitive agreements.
- **Permitted Purpose** — The defined scope within which disclosed confidential information may be used, here limited to negotiating a long-term GoO and potential power supply agreement.
- **Affiliate** — An entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a contracting party, typically defined by a 50% voting capital threshold.
- **Offshore wind GoO sourcing** — The procurement of Guarantees of Origin specifically linked to generation from a named offshore wind farm (Borssele 3 & 4).
