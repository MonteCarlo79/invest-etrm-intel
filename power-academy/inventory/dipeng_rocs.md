## Concepts
- **Renewables Obligation Certificate (ROC)** — A tradeable certificate issued per MWh of qualifying renewable generation, used by suppliers to demonstrate compliance with the Renewables Obligation.
- **Feed-in Tariff (FiT)** — A government-guaranteed per-kWh payment to small-scale renewable generators, fixed for 20 years and RPI-linked.
- **ROC banding** — Technology- and configuration-specific multipliers that determine how many ROCs are issued per MWh of generation.
- **Buy-out price** — The RPI-indexed fixed price per ROC that suppliers pay Ofgem when they cannot present sufficient ROCs to meet their Renewables Obligation.
- **Recycle payment** — A redistribution of buy-out fund proceeds back to generators who presented ROCs, calculated annually in proportion to ROCs presented.
- **ROC auction price** — The market-clearing price at which ROCs are sold via auction, subject to fees and demand dynamics.
- **ROC forward curve** — A term structure of expected future ROC prices used for deal valuation and risk quantification.
- **Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)** — A bilateral contract between a renewable generator and a supplier specifying the terms for purchasing ROCs and associated electricity.
- **Export tariff** — An RPI-linked guaranteed rate paid to FiT-accredited generators for electricity exported to the grid.
- **Return on Average Capital Employed (RoACE)** — An annualised profitability metric applied to trading working capital to assess deal economics on a normalised basis.
- **Potential Future Exposure (PFE)** — A credit-risk measure estimating the maximum likely counterparty exposure over the life of a trade at a given confidence level.
- **Ofgem accreditation** — The regulatory approval process by which a generator qualifies to receive ROCs or FiTs under the relevant government scheme.

## Methods
- ROC revenue calculation (banding × MWh × auction/buy-out price)
- Annualised working-capital RoACE calculation
- PFE estimation for ROC purchase deals
- FiT tiered-rate lookup and annual earnings projection
- ROI and payback-period estimation for renewable installations
- ROC forward curve construction
