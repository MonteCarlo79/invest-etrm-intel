## Concepts
- **Cross-border interconnection points** — Physical or virtual locations where gas transmission capacity is offered between neighbouring TSO systems across national borders.
- **Maximum technical capacity** — The maximum firm capacity that a TSO can offer at an interconnection point in a given flow direction.
- **Bidirectional flow capability** — Classification of interconnection points by whether firm or virtual reverse capacity can be offered in both directions simultaneously.
- **Virtual backhaul capacity** — Commercially offered reverse-flow capacity at a point that is physically unidirectional, enabled through netting or nomination offsets.
- **Trading points and market areas** — Virtual hubs or balancing zones where gas title transfers between shippers, identified by area codes such as TTF, NBP, PSV, and GASPOOL.
- **LNG terminal entry points** — Connection locations at which regasified LNG is injected into the onshore transmission system, with associated technical capacities.
- **Gross calorific value (GCV)** — Energy content per unit volume of gas used to convert physical flow quantities between volumetric and energy units at interconnection points.
- **Transmission system operators (TSOs)** — Entities responsible for operating national or regional high-pressure gas transport networks and publishing capacity data at interconnection points.
- **Gas storage facility types** — Classification of underground storage assets—salt cavern, aquifer, depleted field—that connect to the transmission system.
- **ENTSOG Ten-Year Network Development Plan (TYNDP)** — Forward-looking infrastructure plan listing existing and planned pipeline and LNG projects across the ENTSOG member area.
- **Primary market capacity** — Firm and interruptible transmission capacity auctioned directly by TSOs at interconnection points on the regulated primary market.
- **Summer capacity** — Additional technical capacity available at specific interconnection points only during the summer months due to reduced domestic demand.
