## Concepts
- **Average Dispatch** — The expected generation output of a plant computed by averaging dispatch decisions across simulated price scenarios.
- **Delta Hedging** — A hedging strategy that locks in forward exposure equal to the expected dispatch quantity of the plant.
- **Clean Spark Spread** — The net margin of a gas-fired power plant after accounting for fuel and carbon costs, expressed per MWh.
- **Variable Cost** — The per-MWh operating cost of a plant used to determine whether dispatch is profitable in a given price scenario.
- **Simulated Price Paths** — A set of Monte Carlo price realisations used to evaluate the distribution of plant dispatch and value.
- **Plant Optionality** — The economic value arising from the plant owner's right but not obligation to generate in each period.

## Methods
- Monte Carlo simulation
- Scenario-based dispatch optimisation
- Delta calculation from simulated paths
- Payoff averaging across scenarios
