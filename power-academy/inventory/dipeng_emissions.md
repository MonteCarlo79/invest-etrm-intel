## Concepts
- **Cap-and-Trade** — A regulatory mechanism setting an overall emissions ceiling within which covered entities may trade allowances to meet compliance obligations cost-effectively.
- **Emissions Allowance Allocation** — The process by which regulators distribute emissions permits to covered entities, including free allocation (grandfathering), auctioning, and benchmarking approaches.
- **Grandfathering** — Allocation of free emissions permits based on an entity's historical emission levels rather than on competitive bidding or performance benchmarks.
- **Benchmarking Allocation** — Setting permit entitlements by reference to an output- or activity-based efficiency standard rather than historical emissions.
- **Carbon Price** — The market-determined price of an emissions allowance that signals the marginal cost of abatement across covered installations.
- **Carbon Price Volatility** — Fluctuations in allowance prices arising from over-allocation, fuel-switching dynamics, and regulatory uncertainty within the ETS.
- **Windfall Profits** — Unintended gains accruing to energy producers who receive free allowances yet pass the opportunity cost of those allowances through to consumers in output prices.
- **Aviation Inclusion in ETS** — The policy extension of the EU ETS to cover CO2 emissions from air transport, with specific permit allocation challenges for different carrier types.
- **International Emissions Trading** — Cross-border exchange of emissions permits between industrialised and developing countries to achieve abatement at lower global cost.
- **Offset Credits (CCER)** — Emission reduction units generated by certified mitigation projects that covered entities may surrender in lieu of capped allowances.
- **PM2.5 and PM10 Particulate Matter** — Size-classified airborne particles regulated under EU air quality directives on account of their documented adverse effects on human health.
- **Chemical Transport Models** — Computational frameworks that simulate emissions dispersal, atmospheric chemistry, and pollutant transformation to produce air quality forecasts.
- **Reactive Nitrogen Compounds (RNC)** — A class of nitrogen-containing pollutants from combustion, including NOx and ammonia, subject to emissions ceilings under EU and UN-ECE protocols.
- **Epidemiological Exposure Assessment** — Quantification of population-level exposure to air pollutants to establish statistical associations between pollution concentrations and health outcomes.
- **Low Emission Zones** — Geographically defined urban areas where vehicle access is restricted to reduce local traffic-related air pollution concentrations.
- **Emissions Trading System (ETS)** — A cap-and-trade or intensity-based market mechanism requiring covered emitters to hold allowances equal to their verified emissions.
- **Quota Allocation** — The process by which regulatory authorities distribute emission allowances to covered entities, covering free allocation, auctioning, and grandfathering approaches.
- **Intensity-Based Reduction Target** — A carbon mitigation approach that caps emissions relative to output rather than imposing an absolute emissions ceiling.
- **Absolute Emissions Cap** — A hard upper limit on total greenhouse gas emissions within a trading system, as distinct from intensity-based benchmarks.
- **Market Liquidity** — The ease with which allowances can be bought and sold, measured by traded volume relative to total quota and turnover rates.
- **Compliance Period and Surrender Obligation** — The regulatory cycle within which covered entities must submit allowances equal to their verified emissions to avoid penalties.
- **Monitoring, Reporting and Verification (MRV)** — The standardised framework for measuring, disclosing, and independently checking greenhouse gas emissions of covered entities.
- **China Certified Emission Reduction (CCER)** — Domestic voluntary offset credits generated under approved methodologies that covered entities may use for a capped share of their compliance obligation.
- **Market Participant Eligibility** — Rules determining which entities—covered emitters, institutional investors, foreign participants—may open accounts and trade in a carbon market.
- **Price Discovery** — The process by which allowance prices are formed through continuous trading, auction, or negotiated block transfer mechanisms.
- **Offset Mechanism** — Provisions allowing covered entities to substitute a proportion of required allowances with certified emission reductions from approved project types.
- **Enforcement and Penalties** — Regulatory powers exercised by provincial and national authorities to sanction non-compliant entities through fines, administrative measures, or criminal prosecution.
- **Two-Tier Regulatory Structure** — Governance architecture dividing carbon market oversight between the national NDRC (policy and standards) and provincial DRCs (implementation and enforcement).
- **Registry and Settlement System** — The electronic infrastructure recording allowance holdings, transfers, surrenders, and cancellations and linking to exchange trading systems.
- **Benchmark Allowance Methodology (Grandfathering vs. Benchmarking)** — Alternative methods for setting entity-level free allocations, either from historical emission levels or from sectoral efficiency standards.
- **Carbon Finance Products** — Financial instruments—including pledged loans, repurchase agreements, bond structures, and asset management funds—that use carbon allowances or CCERs as underlying assets.
- **Market Transparency** — Obligations on exchanges and regulators to disclose prices, volumes, allocation data, and compliance outcomes to market participants.
- **Pilot-to-National Market Transition** — The policy and operational process of migrating regional pilot ETS schemes into a unified national carbon market framework.
- **Negotiated Block Transfer (协议转让)** — An over-the-counter trade mode for large-volume allowance transactions concluded bilaterally within an exchange-set price band.
- **Position Limits and Large-Holder Reporting** — Risk controls capping the maximum allowances held by any single participant and requiring disclosure when holdings approach defined thresholds.
- **Emissions Allowance (配额)** — Government-issued permit granting a covered entity the right to emit one tonne of CO2 within a defined compliance period.
- **Compliance Obligation (履约)** — Requirement for covered emitters to surrender allowances equal to their verified annual emissions by a specified deadline.
- **Cap Setting and Quota Allocation** — Administrative process by which authorities determine total annual emission caps and distribute allowances to covered entities.
- **Chinese Certified Emissions Reductions (CCERs)** — Domestic voluntary offset credits certified by the National Development and Reform Commission that can be used to partially fulfil compliance obligations.
- **Offset Usage Limits** — Regulatory ceiling—typically 5%—on the proportion of a covered entity's compliance obligation that may be met with offset credits rather than allowances.
- **Registry System (注册登记簿)** — Official electronic ledger that records the holding, transfer, cancellation, and retirement of emissions allowances and offsets.
- **Third-Party Verification (第三方核查)** — Independent audit of a covered entity's emissions report conducted by an accredited verification body before allowances are issued or surrendered.
- **Open-Market Operations (公开市场操作)** — Regulator-initiated allowance auctions or buy-backs used to dampen excessive price volatility and maintain orderly market conditions.
- **Price Controls and Circuit Breakers** — Exchange rules that suspend or restrict trading when allowance prices move beyond defined percentage bands within a session or across consecutive days.
- **Position Limits (持仓限制)** — Maximum allowance volumes that a single participant may hold or transact in a single session, designed to prevent market concentration.
- **Auction Mechanism (配额拍卖)** — Sealed-bid, uniform-price auction process by which regulators sell reserve allowances to compliance entities and other approved participants.
- **Buy-back / Repurchase (配额回购)** — Regulator-funded purchase of allowances from the secondary market when prices fall below a prescribed minimum to support the carbon price floor.
- **Margin / Performance Bond (诚信保证金)** — Financial deposit lodged with the exchange by participants to cover potential settlement obligations arising from their trading activity.
- **OTC / Negotiated Transfer (协议转让)** — Bilaterally negotiated allowance transaction conducted outside the exchange order book, subject to separate documentation and registration requirements.
- **Block Trade Rules (大宗交易)** — Special procedures applicable when a single trade order exceeds a defined volume threshold, requiring off-screen negotiation and subsequent exchange registration.
- **T+3 Settlement** — Allowance delivery and cash payment completion three trading days after the trade date, as prescribed by Beijing exchange rules.
- **Administrative Penalty Discretion (行政处罚自由裁量权)** — Structured framework specifying graduated penalty bands for non-compliance, including reporting failures and over-allocation emissions.
- **KYC / Counterparty Approval** — Requirement for a market participant to vet and approve trading counterparties before executing transactions, including blocking unapproved members.
- **Exit Put Option on Pilot Allowances** — Contractual right entitling a counterparty to sell pilot-market allowances back to Shell at a guaranteed floor price (CNY 25), synthetically replicating a put option payoff.
- **Pilot-to-National Allowance Conversion** — Mechanism by which pilot-scheme allowances held at the end of the pilot period are exchanged for national ETS allowances at a regulatory-determined conversion ratio.
- **Asian (Average-Price) Put Option** — Path-dependent option whose payoff is based on the average settlement price of allowances over a period rather than the price on a single expiry date.
- **Lock-in Period and Liquidity Strategy** — Contractual holding period during which a counterparty cannot freely sell allowances except under defined conditions, used to manage supply timing and price targets.
- **Trader-Assessed Probability (vprob)** — Subjective probability assigned by a trader to the volume of allowances that will trade at or above a target price during the lock-in period, used to value optionality.
- **Carbon Price Floor / CNY 20 Guarantee** — Contractual guarantee by Shell to compensate counterparties if converted national allowances' average price falls below CNY 20 per tonne.
- **Anti-Bribery and Anti-Money Laundering Compliance** — Contractual obligations requiring exchange members to comply with applicable AML and anti-bribery laws in connection with allowance transactions.
- **Pilot Carbon Allowances (SHEA/GDEA)** — Tradeable emission permits issued under China's Shanghai and Guangdong pilot emissions trading schemes.
- **National Allowance Conversion** — The regulatory process by which pilot scheme allowances are exchanged for allowances under the forthcoming Chinese national ETS at a regulator-set conversion ratio.
- **Exit Put Option (CNY25-strike)** — An embedded option granting the dealer the right to sell accumulated allowances back at a fixed CNY strike price, arising from volume-weighted trading mechanics during the lock-in period.
- **CNY20-strike Asian Put** — An average-price put option on national allowances, implicitly written by the dealer as a price floor guarantee to counterparts on converted volumes.
- **Lock-in Period** — A 20-month window during which counterpart allowance positions are constrained and on-screen trading volumes determine option payoff triggers.
- **vprob (Probability-weighted Volume)** — The trader-assessed volume of pilot allowances expected to be sold at or above CNY25, used to parameterise deal value sensitivities.
- **Conversion Ratio (c)** — The regulator-determined ratio of national allowances received per pilot allowance surrendered, affecting residual position size and hedging requirements.
- **Deal Value Decomposition (Values 1–5)** — A structured breakdown of total deal PnL into component sources including spread income, screen-trading fees, interest carry, put premium capture, and conversion margin.
- **PNational (Average National Allowance Price)** — The simple average of national allowance closing prices from conversion date to 30 June 2018, used as the settlement reference for converted volume payoffs.
- **Delta-Hedging with Forward National Allowances** — Selling forward national allowances to offset directional price exposure when probability-weighted volume falls short of the full position.
- **Dynamic Hedging of Average-Price Guarantee** — Daily pro-rata forward sales of residual national allowance volumes to replicate a delta-hedge against the Asian put exposure embedded in the price guarantee.
- **FX Sensitivity (USD/CNY)** — Linear dependence of deal values on the USD/CNY exchange rate arising from USD-denominated investment into CNY-denominated allowances.
- **Allowance Price Seasonality** — Recurring intra-year price patterns in Chinese carbon markets exploited by timing allowance sales around conversion and settlement windows.
- **Stress-Testing and Breakeven Analysis** — Scenario analysis across conversion ratios and national allowance price levels to determine the PNational breakeven that covers guaranteed floor losses.
- **Implied Volatility of Carbon Allowances** — Market-implied or historically estimated volatility of pilot and national allowance prices, used to price embedded put options.
- **PnL Simulation** — Quantitative modelling of deal cashflows across Monte Carlo or scenario paths to assess expected margin and tail risk.
- **Value at Risk (VaR)** — A statistical measure of potential deal loss used to size and stress-test the early access structure positions.
