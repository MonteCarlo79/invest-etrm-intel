## Concepts
- **AOM Baseload Delta** — The option delta for at-the-money baseload power options used as the hedge ratio for physical or financial dispatch.
- **Peak Incremental Delta** — The marginal delta contribution attributable to peak-period exposure, distinguishing peak from baseload hedge requirements.
- **Delta Hedging** — The practice of matching option delta to physical dispatch quantities to neutralise first-order price risk.
- **Dispatch Comparison** — Side-by-side analysis of modelled dispatch quantities versus theoretical delta values to identify mismatches.
- **Pre-Correction vs Post-Correction Analysis** — Evaluation of delta and dispatch alignment before and after applying adjustments to isolate and quantify modelling or operational errors.
- **Baseload vs Peak Decomposition** — Separation of total power position into flat baseload and incremental peak components for granular hedging.
