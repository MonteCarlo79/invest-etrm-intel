## Concepts
- **Fundamental (Stack) Model** — A market-level dispatch model that minimises total generation costs to produce price and generation forecasts.
- **Optimisation Model** — A plant-level model that maximises asset operating value given exogenous price inputs and plant operating constraints.
- **Short-Run Marginal Cost (SRMC)** — The variable cost of producing one additional unit of electricity, used to order plant in the merit stack.
- **Merit Order / Supply Stack** — The ranking of generation units by marginal cost to determine dispatch sequence and market clearing price.
- **Marginal Cost Pricing** — The principle that the market clearing price equals the SRMC of the last unit dispatched to meet demand.
- **Asset Valuation** — The process of estimating the operating value of a power plant or tolling agreement using dispatch optimisation.
- **Tolling Agreement** — A contractual arrangement valued by the optimisation model that grants rights to dispatch a plant against given fuel and power prices.
- **Plant Operating Constraints** — Technical limits on a generation unit including start-up costs and ramp-up/ramp-down rates that bound feasible dispatch.
- **Interconnector Availability** — A capacity constraint representing cross-border transmission limits included in fundamental dispatch models.
- **NPV Approach** — A discounted cash-flow valuation method noted as a limitation of fundamental models for not capturing optionality.
- **Carbon Cost** — The cost of CO2 emissions allowances included as a fuel-related input in both fundamental and optimisation models.
- **Power Demand Forecast** — A projected load profile used as the key demand-side input to fundamental dispatch models.

## Methods
- Merit order stack construction
- Cost minimisation (fundamental model objective)
- Profit/value maximisation (optimisation model objective)
- NPV-based asset valuation
- Dispatch pattern simulation
