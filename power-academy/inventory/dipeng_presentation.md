## Concepts
- **Emissions Trading Scheme (ETS) pilot programme** — Regional cap-and-trade schemes selected by NDRC across seven Chinese cities/provinces as precursors to a national market.
- **Chinese Certified Emission Reduction (CCER)** — Domestic project-based offset mechanism generating credits eligible for compliance use within China's pilot ETS schemes.
- **Price controls on exchange trading** — Regulatory limits on permissible trade prices that restrict the formation of forward or large-volume transactions on Chinese emissions exchanges.
- **Central Counterparty (CCP)** — An entity that interposes itself between buyer and seller to guarantee trade settlement and enable anonymous execution with margining.
- **Exchange membership and trading access** — Structural constraints on the number of login credentials per member that limit simultaneous trading and compliance monitoring.
- **Know Your Customer (KYC) in anonymous exchange markets** — The challenge of satisfying counterparty identification requirements when exchange spot trades are executed anonymously pre- and post-deal.
- **OTC versus exchange spot trading** — Distinction between bilaterally negotiated over-the-counter transactions and exchange-executed spot trades in emissions allowances.
- **Forward trading with cleared CCP settlement** — Exchange-listed forward contracts on emissions allowances novated and margined through a central clearing house.
- **Minimum volume commitment** — A negotiated trading obligation whereby a participant guarantees a threshold annual volume in exchange for reduced exchange fees.
