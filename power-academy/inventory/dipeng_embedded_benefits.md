## Concepts
- **Embedded Generation** — Generation connected to distribution networks rather than the transmission system, potentially exempt from certain charges below 100 MW capacity.
- **Transmission Network Use of System (TNUoS) Charges** — Charges levied on generators and suppliers to recover allowed revenues of Transmission Owners, split into locational and residual elements.
- **Demand TNUoS Netting** — The mechanism by which a supplier's gross demand is reduced by embedded generation output for the purpose of calculating TNUoS liability.
- **Generation TNUoS Avoidance** — The benefit to sub-100 MW embedded generators of not paying generation TNUoS charges that apply to transmission-connected generators.
- **TNUoS Locational Element** — The component of TNUoS tariff that reflects the cost of the wider transmission system and varies by geographic location.
- **TNUoS Residual Element** — The component added to TNUoS tariffs to ensure correct revenue recovery from generation and demand users beyond the locational signal.
- **Balancing Services Use of System (BSUoS) Charges** — Per-MWh charges on both generation and demand users to recover the costs of operating the transmission system, including transmission losses.
- **Embedded BSUoS Benefit** — The double avoidance of BSUoS charges accruing to embedded generators and their associated suppliers under current BSC arrangements.
- **Transmission Losses** — Energy losses incurred in transmitting electricity across the transmission network, recovered via adjustments to users' energy accounts under the BSC.
- **Embedded Losses Benefit** — The reduction in a supplier's transmission loss liability arising from netting embedded generation against demand, valued at twice the absolute loss charge.
- **Balancing and Settlement Code (BSC)** — The GB industry code governing balancing, settlement, and registration of parties including embedded generators and their access to the national energy account.
- **Grid Supply Point (GSP)** — The metering point at which electricity flows from the transmission system into the distribution network, used as the basis for TNUoS demand charging.
- **Licence Exemption** — The regulatory status of embedded generators below 100 MW capacity that exempts them from generation TNUoS obligations.

- BSUoS per-MWh charge quantification
- Transmission loss adjustment via BSC energy accounts
