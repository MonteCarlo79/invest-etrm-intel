## Concepts
- **Day-Ahead Moving Average (DA MA) Prices** — Captures the moving-average structure of day-ahead power prices to separate systematic profile from noise.
- **Imbalance Cost Analysis** — Quantifies the costs arising from deviations between scheduled and actual generation or consumption in the balancing market.
- **Hourly DA Weighted Revenue** — Measures portfolio revenue by weighting day-ahead prices against hourly generation or position profiles.
- **Bias Correction in Price Statistics** — Adjusts raw price model outputs to remove systematic forecast bias identified in historical data.
- **Historical Volatility** — Estimates the degree of price variability observed in past power and gas market data over a defined lookback window.
- **Power-Gas Correlation Collapse** — Describes periods where the normally positive statistical relationship between power and gas prices breaks down significantly.
- **Cross-Commodity Correlation** — Measures the co-movement between power and natural gas prices for use in joint risk and pricing models.
- **Dutch NZW Pricing Model** — A region-specific day-ahead price model calibrated to Dutch North-West zone market conditions.
