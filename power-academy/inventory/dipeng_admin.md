## Concepts
- **Power Valuation Template** — Standardised modelling framework covering Wind/PPA, spark spreads, merchant tolls, shaped products and aggregator book reviews.
- **Physical Power Dispatch Model** — Asset-level dispatch simulation used to price and value power generation positions.
- **Power I&C Pricing** — Pricing methodology for industrial and commercial electricity supply contracts.
- **Power Swing Valuation** — Valuation of volume flexibility embedded in power supply or offtake agreements.
- **Wind PPA Valuation** — Mark-to-market and structuring framework for power purchase agreements linked to wind generation.
- **Spark Spread** — Margin measure capturing the difference between power revenue and gas fuel cost for a generation asset.
- **Merchant Toll** — Contractual arrangement granting the right to convert gas to power at a fixed tolling fee without owning the asset.
- **Long-Dated Gas Sales Valuation** — Curve verification, hedging methodology and credit review process applied to multi-year gas supply transactions.
- **Gas Transaction Reshaping** — Restructuring existing gas sales or storage deals to alter volume profiles and avoid tender obligations.
- **Storage Arbitrage** — Strategy exploiting seasonal or locational price spreads through injection and withdrawal of gas in storage facilities.
- **Aggregator Book Acquisition** — Process of evaluating and acquiring energy supply portfolios from aggregators including DS book simplification.
- **Mark-to-Market (MTM)** — Current fair-value measurement of open deal positions against prevailing market prices.
- **Lcal Premium Socialisation** — Regulatory mechanism spreading locational capacity costs across market participants affecting pricing approaches.
- **Transport Tariff Structuring** — Regulatory and commercial rules governing daily and term gas transportation charges and their impact on deal economics.
- **Curve Verification** — Independent validation of forward price curves used as inputs to deal valuation models.
