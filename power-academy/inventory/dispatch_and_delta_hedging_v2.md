## Concepts
- **Average Dispatch** — The mean of plant output across simulated price paths, computed by averaging dispatch decisions at each simulated price realisation.
- **Delta Hedging** — The sensitivity of a plant's expected value to a parallel shift in the forward price, used to determine the hedge volume.
- **Clean Spark Spread (CSS)** — The net margin per MWh for a gas-fired plant after subtracting fuel and carbon costs from the power price, used as the dispatch signal.
- **Monte Carlo Price Simulation** — A set of simulated forward price paths used to compute expected dispatch and expected value of a generation asset.
- **Expected Value (EV)** — The probability-weighted average of revenues generated by optimally dispatching a plant across all simulated price scenarios.
- **Value from Average Dispatch** — An approximation that applies the mean dispatch to the mean price, which is mathematically distinct from true expected value due to the correlation between dispatch and price.
- **Price Shift Sensitivity** — The change in expected plant value resulting from a uniform upward shift applied to all simulated prices, used to numerically estimate delta.
- **Intrinsic Dispatch** — The on/off dispatch decision based on whether the simulated spread exceeds the plant's variable cost, with no start-up or ramp constraints.
