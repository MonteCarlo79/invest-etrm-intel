## Concepts
- **Power Options Pricing** — Valuation of optionality embedded in electricity contracts using model-driven pricing frameworks.
- **Power Curve Modelling** — Construction and calibration of forward power price curves for use in derivatives pricing.
- **Storage Valuation** — Modelling and optimisation of energy storage assets to extract value from price spreads over time.
- **Intrinsic and Extrinsic Value** — Decomposition of an energy option's total value into its deterministic intrinsic component and stochastic extrinsic component.
- **Options Portfolio Management** — Tracking, aggregation and risk management of a book of power options positions.
- **Greeks and Sensitivities** — Calculation of first- and higher-order price sensitivities for power option positions to support hedging.
- **Spark Spread and Dark Spread Options** — Spread option structures linking power prices to fuel input costs for generation assets.
- **Virtual Power Plant (VPP) Options** — Structured option products that replicate the output optionality of a physical generation asset.
- **Monte Carlo Simulation for Energy Derivatives** — Stochastic simulation of price paths used to price path-dependent and multi-asset power options.
- **Rolling Hedge and Delta Hedging** — Dynamic replication strategies applied to power options books using forward contract adjustments.
