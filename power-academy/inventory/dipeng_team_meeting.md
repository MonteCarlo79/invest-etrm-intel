## Concepts
- **Clean Spark Spread** — The margin from generating power from gas after accounting for fuel cost and carbon cost.
- **Spark Spread Call Option** — An option giving the right to receive the positive difference between power price and gas-equivalent cost above a strike.
- **Spark Spread Put Option** — An option giving the right to receive the positive difference between a strike and the spark spread, used here out-of-the-money.
- **Daily Optionality** — The additional value arising from the ability to exercise an option on a daily rather than a period-average basis.
- **Index Discount with Floor** — A pricing structure where the underlying index is reduced by a discount but bounded below by a minimum floor level.
- **Kirk Model** — An analytical approximation for pricing options on the spread between two correlated forward prices.
- **Power-Gas Correlation** — The statistical relationship between power and gas prices that drives spark spread option value and hedge effectiveness.
- **Implied Volatility Calibration** — The process of inferring market-consistent volatility parameters from observed option prices.
- **Curve Profiling** — The construction of granular forward price curves capturing intra-period shape from coarser market quotes.
- **Backtesting** — The retrospective assessment of a valuation or hedging strategy against historical market data.
- **Market Liquidity Risk** — The risk that insufficient trading volume impairs the ability to price, hedge or exit positions reliably.
