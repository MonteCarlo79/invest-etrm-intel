## Concepts
- **Baseload power product** — Standardised electricity contract delivering flat power across all hours of the delivery period.
- **Peak power product** — Standardised electricity contract delivering power only during defined peak hours of the delivery period.
- **Daily granularity** — Time resolution at which power price or volume data is recorded or settled on a day-by-day basis.
- **Market data collector** — Structured template or tool that aggregates price and related data from multiple sources into a single repository.
- **Cross-market data organisation** — Systematic arrangement of data across different national electricity markets within a unified framework.
- **PeGN (Peak German-style)** — Peak contract convention used in French power markets referencing German peak-hour definitions.

## Methods
- Spreadsheet-based data aggregation
- Product segmentation by load type (baseload vs peak)
- Geographic segmentation by market (DE, FR)
- Daily time-series structuring
