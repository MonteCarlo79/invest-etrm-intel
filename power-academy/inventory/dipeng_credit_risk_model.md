## Concepts
- **Mark-to-Market (M2M)** — Current fair value of an open derivatives position used as input to credit exposure calculations.
- **Potential Future Exposure (PFE)** — Statistical measure of worst-case future credit exposure of a derivatives portfolio over a given horizon.
- **Credit Risk Model Versioning** — Iterative refinement of a credit exposure calculation model tracked across numbered releases.
