## Concepts
- **Energy Management Partnership** — A structured commercial arrangement whereby a trading counterparty provides market access, optimisation, and capital services to a generation asset owner.
- **Clean Spark Spread (CSS)** — The margin of a gas-fired generator after deducting fuel and carbon costs from the power sale price.
- **Tolling Agreement** — A contract granting a counterparty the right to dispatch a generation asset in exchange for a premium, transferring commodity price risk to the toller.
- **Virtual Power Plant (VPP)** — A financial or physical construct that replicates generation asset optionality without direct ownership of the plant.
- **CSS Option (Daily/Seasonal)** — An option whose payoff is linked to the clean spark spread, exercisable on a daily or seasonal basis to capture CCGT dispatch optionality.
- **CSS Put Option** — A derivative instrument providing the generator with downside margin protection when the clean spark spread falls below a strike level.
- **Short-Term Optimisation** — The active trading of intraday, day-ahead, and near-forward markets to extract incremental value from generation asset flexibility.
- **Long-Term Value Optimisation** — Multi-year structural arrangements such as tolling or VPPs designed to monetise asset optionality and stabilise cash flows over an extended horizon.
- **Merchant Operation** — Generation capacity operated without long-term hedging, with output optimised opportunistically against prevailing spot and short-term market prices.
- **Gas Supply Agreement (PEG-N linked)** — A physical gas supply contract indexed to the Powernext PEG-N day-ahead price providing volume flexibility without take-or-pay obligations.
- **Capacity Certificates Market** — A French regulatory mechanism creating tradeable certificates representing committed generation capacity to ensure system adequacy.
- **Balancing Responsibility** — The obligation of a market participant to ensure that energy injections and withdrawals remain in balance within a defined settlement period.
- **Profit Sharing Mechanism** — A contractual feature allowing the asset owner to participate in upside margins above a pre-specified spread level alongside the trading counterparty.
- **Mezzanine Debt** — A hybrid capital instrument ranking between senior debt and equity, used here as a potential capital provision structure within the partnership.
- **Working Capital Facility** — A credit arrangement embedded in trading or tolling agreements to fund operational liquidity requirements of the generation asset.
- **EFET M+1 D20 Payment Terms** — Standard European Federation of Energy Traders settlement terms specifying payment 20 days after the end of the delivery month.
- **Extension Option (Tolling)** — A pre-agreed right embedded in a tolling contract allowing one party to extend the agreement at-the-money beyond the initial term at a premium fixed at inception.
- **Spark/Dark/Country Spreads** — Margin measures for gas-fired and coal-fired generation respectively, and cross-border power price differentials used as trading benchmarks.
