## Concepts
- **Portfolio detail line-by-line trade capture** — Enumerates individual trades within an acquired book to establish a verified position inventory.
- **Credit charge** — Quantifies the cost of counterparty credit risk embedded in open bilateral energy trades.
- **Portfolio economics** — Aggregates mark-to-market values, margin requirements and net present value across a transferred trade book.
- **Masterfile reconciliation** — Cross-references trade-level data against a master position record to detect discrepancies or missing legs.
- **Flat trades** — Identifies offsetting or zero-net-exposure positions that can be novated or cancelled at minimal cost.
- **Book summary valuation** — Condenses portfolio-level P&L, credit, and risk metrics into an executive-facing snapshot for deal assessment.
- **Novation and trade transfer** — Covers the legal and operational process of transferring contractual obligations from seller to buyer in an energy book acquisition.
- **Credit-adjusted NPV** — Adjusts the risk-free net present value of a trade book for expected counterparty default losses.
