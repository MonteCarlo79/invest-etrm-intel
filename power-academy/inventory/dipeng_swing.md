## Concepts
- **Swing option** — A commodity derivative granting the holder the right to vary daily take quantities within contractual limits over a delivery period.
- **Monte Carlo simulation for swing valuation** — Stochastic simulation of price paths used to estimate the expected value of swing exercise strategies.
- **Play optimisation** — Determination of the optimal exercise strategy for a swing contract given simulated forward price scenarios.
- **Market-to-market / mark-to-market (MA-DA) valuation** — Daily valuation of swing positions by comparing market price to contract strike across historical periods.
- **Fixed random-number simulation** — Use of a fixed random seed or pre-drawn random numbers to ensure reproducibility of Monte Carlo paths.
- **Gas swing standalone model** — Self-contained valuation model for natural gas swing contracts without cross-commodity linkage.
- **Power swing standalone model** — Self-contained valuation model for electricity swing contracts without cross-commodity linkage.
- **NBP price history** — Historical National Balancing Point gas spot and forward price data used as model inputs.
- **ICE Heren price data** — Exchange-sourced forward curve data for natural gas used in historical back-valuation of swing contracts.
