## Concepts
- **Locational spread option** — An option whose payoff depends on the price difference between two geographic delivery points in a power market.
- **Locational price spread** — The difference in electricity spot prices between two interconnected bidding zones or nodes.
- **Wind power market data** — Time-series records of wind generation output and associated market variables used for quantitative analysis.
- **Wholesale electricity pricing** — The determination and recording of clearing prices in organised day-ahead and intraday power markets.
- **Bidding zone interconnection** — The cross-border capacity linking separate pricing zones that gives rise to locational price differentials.
- **Market statistics and descriptive analytics** — Summary statistical measures applied to power price and generation time-series to characterise distributional properties.
