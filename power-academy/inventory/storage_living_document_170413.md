## Concepts
- **Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS)** — Net-present-value-based metric expressing the full lifecycle cost of an electricity storage facility per unit of energy output, analogous to LCOE for generation.
- **Value Stacking** — Strategy of maximising storage asset utilisation and revenue by simultaneously delivering multiple grid or behind-the-meter services.
- **Behind-the-Meter Storage** — Storage sited downstream of the grid connection point, enabling location-specific services such as demand charge reduction and PV self-consumption increase.
- **Centralized Grid Storage** — Large-scale storage connected at transmission or distribution level, optimised for system-wide services rather than site-specific benefits.
- **Techno-Economic Analysis** — Quantitative assessment combining technical performance parameters and cost data to evaluate storage competitiveness and deployment conditions.
- **Storage Services** — Range of grid and customer functions that storage can provide, including frequency regulation, peak shaving, and renewable energy time-shifting.
- **Frequency Containment Reserves** — Fast-response ancillary service maintaining grid frequency within limits, historically provided by power plants and increasingly contestable by storage.
- **Demand Charge Reduction** — Behind-the-meter service whereby storage lowers a customer's peak power demand to reduce tariff charges based on maximum consumption.
- **PV Self-Consumption** — Use of on-site battery storage to increase the share of locally generated solar electricity consumed at the point of generation rather than exported.
- **Microgrid Optimisation** — Modelling approach that determines the least-cost combination of distributed generation and storage assets within an islanded or semi-islanded local energy system.
- **Storage Technology Characteristics** — Techno-economic and safety parameters—including power capacity cost, energy capacity cost, and HSSE properties—that differentiate storage technologies.
- **CAPEX and OPEX of Storage** — Capital and annual operating expenditure components that together determine the total cost base entering an LCOS or NPV calculation.
- **Multi-Year Cash Flow Analysis** — Project-level financial model that captures time-varying revenues and costs across a storage asset's life, accounting for regulatory changes and market evolution.
- **Regulatory Impact on Storage** — Ways in which rules, tariffs, subsidies, and market access restrictions can prohibit, disincentivise, or incentivise electricity storage deployment.
- **Flexibility in Power Systems** — Capability to balance supply and demand across timescales from milliseconds to years, provided by storage, interconnection, flexible plant, and demand response.
- **Storage Cost Database** — Structured repository of current and forecast cost, deployment, and market data for storage technologies used to inform project and investment assessments.
- **National Energy System Modelling** — Long-horizon whole-system models used to assess the role of storage within national energy transitions out to 2050.
