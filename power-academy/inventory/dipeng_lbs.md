## Concepts
- **European gas storage balances** — Covers the role of seasonal storage levels in setting European gas price dynamics and supply overhang.
- **TTF hub liquidity and churn rate** — Covers the growth of TTF as Europe's dominant traded gas hub measured by traded volume relative to NBP and churn rate.
- **Gas price drivers** — Covers the supply-side, demand-side, and structural factors that determine wholesale gas prices in Europe.
- **LNG supply growth and Atlantic Basin expansion** — Covers the wave of new liquefaction capacity, particularly US LNG, and its impact on global and European gas balances.
- **Europe as global gas balancing market** — Covers Europe's function as the intersection of global LNG and pipeline supply flows that absorbs surplus volumes.
- **Oil-indexed versus hub-indexed gas contracts** — Covers the transition in long-term supply contracts from oil-price linkage toward index and fixed-price structures at traded hubs.
- **Gas-to-power dynamics** — Covers how coal plant retirements, CO2 costs, and gas supply overhang determine gas consumption in power generation.
- **Renewables integration and intermittency** — Covers the impact of wind and solar growth on daily power market changes and gas demand patterns.
- **Structured gas and power products** — Covers bespoke commercial structures including storage/transport optionality, tolling agreements, and flexible-volume supply arrangements.
- **Groningen production constraints** — Covers politically motivated permit restrictions on Dutch Groningen field output and their effect on European supply levels.
- **Cross-regional gas trade flows** — Covers the global network of LNG and pipeline trade routes connecting export and import regions by 2020.
- **Market participant landscape in European energy trading** — Covers the changing mix of utilities, banks, industrial users, hedge funds, and LDCs active in gas, power, and emissions markets.
- **REMIT reporting obligations** — Covers EU regulatory requirements for transaction and fundamental data reporting in wholesale power and gas markets.
- **Oil inventory overhang and energy price linkage** — Covers how crude oil stock levels feed through to gas and power commodity price sentiment.
