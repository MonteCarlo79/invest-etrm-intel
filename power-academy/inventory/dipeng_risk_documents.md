## Concepts
- **IRR Ungeared** — Unlevered internal rate of return equating after-tax project cash flows to zero NPV, excluding financing costs.
- **IRR Geared** — Levered internal rate of return comparing Shell net equity investment to after-tax cash flows inclusive of financing costs.
- **RTEP Ungeared** — Real-terms equivalent of the unlevered IRR computed on inflation-adjusted cash flows rather than money-of-the-day flows.
- **RTEP Geared** — Real-terms equivalent of the levered IRR, comparing Shell equity investment to real after-tax cash flows including financing.
- **NPV 7% Real Terms** — Net present value of after-tax real cash flows discounted at the Shell global cost-of-capital premise of 7%.
- **NPV (LIBOR)** — Mark-to-market deal value computed as pre-tax gross margin in money-of-the-day discounted at the forward LIBOR rate.
- **Value Investment Ratio (VIR)** — Ratio of levered after-tax NPV7%RT to the present value of all fixed commitments and obligations at 7% real terms.
- **Value at Risk (VaR)** — Difference between mean NPV(LIBOR) and the 99th-percentile worst-case transaction value over a one-year holding period.
- **Return on Value at Risk (RoVaR)** — Ratio of transaction NPV(LIBOR) to transaction Value at Risk at a 99% confidence interval and one-year holding period.
- **Return on Average Capital Employed (ROACE)** — Ratio of annual average undiscounted NIAT to average capital employed including investments and cash working capital on a money-of-the-day basis.
- **Average Capital Employed (ACE)** — Denominator of ROACE comprising all outstanding investments net of recapture plus cash working capital including margin postings.
- **Real vs Nominal Cash Flows** — Distinction between money-of-the-day and inflation-adjusted cash flows used to reconcile nominal and real return metrics.
- **Group Inflation Premise** — Shell's country-specific inflation assumption used to convert nominal cash flows to real terms for RTEP calculations.
- **Hurdle Rate** — Minimum acceptable return threshold applied to IRR and RTEP metrics relative to borrowing costs or a corporate benchmark.
