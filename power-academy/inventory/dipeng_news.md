- **Trading book divestiture** — Sale of a proprietary or commercial trading book, including its positions and associated contracts, to a third party.
- **Capital-intensive business exposure** — Risk management decision to reduce participation in businesses requiring large capital commitments.
- **Commercial and industrial power supply** — Retail or near-retail supply of electricity and natural gas to commercial and industrial end-users.
- **Commodity firm restructuring** — Strategic scaling back or disposal of business units by a commodity trading house to improve financial position.
